About Meridian

Engineers first. Consultants second.

Founded by a team of licensed structural engineers, Meridian was built on a simple premise: the firm doing the analysis should be the firm that stands behind it. We carry our own professional liability on every stamped drawing, and we staff projects with the same two engineers from concept through construction administration.

No hand-offs to junior teams mid-project. No black-box software you can't audit. Just calculations you could hand to a third-party reviewer tomorrow.
